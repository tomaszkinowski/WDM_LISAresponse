Figure 5 mismatch maps: what was computed and how
=================================================

Script: plotting/investigation_fig5_mismatch_map.py (paper_figures branch).
Each map shows a response model's mismatch against the time-domain reference over
the (f, Delta T) plane that figure 5's validity regions describe.

Files (this folder)
-------------------
  fig5_mismatch_map.pdf                    split_amp_sinc_first, median over each cell's tones
  fig5_mismatch_map_max.pdf                split_amp_sinc_first, largest
  fig5_mismatch_map_production.pdf         production response, median
  fig5_mismatch_map_production_max.pdf     production response, largest
  fig5_mismatch_map{,_max}_L10.pdf         split_amp_sinc_first with the older AMP_LAG_L = 10
                                           (superseded; kept for comparison)
Caches (plotting/cache/): investigation_fig5_mismatch_map.npz,
investigation_fig5_mismatch_map_production.npz (+ _L10). Each stores, for every cell,
tone and tiling, the tone frequency and its mismatch (arrays f, mismatch, shape
(13 cells, 6 offsets, 17 tilings)), plus Nf, f_edges and r.

Response models
---------------
  production            the package's one-arm wavelet response
                        (response_sub.one_arm_response_wdm): one delay per row, the full
                        +-436 s delay carried by the WDM time shift, amplitudes at the
                        landing time t_n - delta_n.
  split_amp_sinc_first  prototypes/delay_rate_correction (response_corrections.py):
                        the constellation centre's Roemer delay n.x_c/c is applied to the
                        waveform itself (tones analytically), so the shift carries only
                        each link's rest delay (-6.6 to -1.7 s, rate <= 4.9e-7). Adds the
                        first-order amplitude-rate and sinc terms and the first-order
                        delay-rate kernel for the rest delay.

Plane covered
-------------
Delta T: figure 6's 17 tilings, Nf = 2 round(logspace(2, 6, 17) / (2 dt)):
  Nf      24   44   80  140  250  444  790 1406 2500 4446 7906 14058 25000 44456 79056 140586 250000
  dT [s]  96  176  320  560 1000 1776 3160 5624  1e4 1.78e4 3.16e4 5.62e4 1e5 1.78e5 3.16e5 5.62e5 1e6
  i.e. Delta T = 96 s to 1e6 s, four per decade; Delta F = 1/(2 Delta T) = 5.2e-3 Hz to 5e-7 Hz.
f: 13 logarithmic cells, five per decade, edges logspace(-4, -1.3, 14) = 1e-4 Hz to 0.0501 Hz;
  cell centres 1.27e-4, 2.05e-4, 3.31e-4, 5.33e-4, 8.60e-4, 1.39e-3, 2.24e-3, 3.61e-3, 5.83e-3,
  9.40e-3, 1.52e-2, 2.45e-2, 3.95e-2 Hz. The plotted f axis runs between the outer cell centres.
  The top is capped at 0.05 Hz by the reference (see below).

Tones per cell
--------------
At each tiling, a cell's tones sit in the band m nearest the cell centre, at
  f = (m + r) Delta F,  r = -5/12, -1/4, -1/12, +1/12, +1/4, +5/12,
i.e. six offsets spread evenly across the band (not including the band centre r = 0 or
the edge r = 1/2). Cells that share a band share their tones. 1092 distinct tones per
model over all tilings.
Blank (grey staircase) cells: a cell is left out at a tiling if any of its tones lies
below 0.75 Delta F, the lower edge of band 1's Meyer window (band 1 minus the window's
A fraction 0.25), where the signal leaks into the dropped m = 0 band. This blanks 39 of
the 221 (cell, tiling) pairs: low f at fine Delta T.

Source and detector
-------------------
  signal        monochromatic, circularly polarised, A_plus = A_cross = 1e-21, phi_0 = 0
                (parameters.MONOCHROMATIC_PARAMS with f replaced)
  sky           Galactic centre, theta = 95.6 deg, phi = 266.9 deg (SSB ecliptic)
  sampling      dt = 4 s, N = 2**23 samples (about 388 days), Tukey taper alpha = 0.05
  start         200 days after the start of the ESA trailing orbit file
                (examples/esa-trailing-orbits.h5)
  link          one link, 1 -> 2
  WDM settings  max_lag_L = 25, num_interp_points P = 128; padding from
                utils.wdm_padding_info for L = 25 and the taper

Reference and comparison
------------------------
  reference     LISAresponse.one_arm_response_time_domain on the same signal, 12
                Gauss-Legendre nodes per link integral, transformed with the same tiling's
                dwt. The waveform is interpolated with 12-tap Lagrange below 0.03 Hz and 20
                taps above: at 12 taps the reference's own error reaches 2e-9 at 0.05 Hz
                (3e-4 at 0.09 Hz), while 20 taps hold it near 1e-12 up to 0.055 Hz. This
                is why the cells stop at 0.05 Hz.
  cut           both grids lose the padding rows, every row holding a tapered sample, and
                the m = 0 band (utils.wdm_cut_info / apply_wdm_cut)
  measure       utils.wdm_mismatch: 1 - |<w1, w2>| / sqrt(<w1, w1><w2, w2>) over the kept
                pixels
  statistic     each cell shows the median (or the largest) mismatch over its six tones

How the maps are drawn
----------------------
  - log10 of the median / largest mismatch, viridis, Gouraud-shaded between cell centres
    (f) and tilings (Delta T); colour range 1e-12 to 1e-2 with an arrow when values go
    above it
  - white contours at 1e-10, 1e-8, 1e-6, 1e-4
  - light-grey staircase: the blank m = 0 leak cells, drawn on the cell edges (geometric
    midpoints between tilings)
  - colour bar on top; right axis Delta F = 1/(2 Delta T)

Runs
----
All computed on calx038 (CPU, 4 processes of 5 cores each, tilings dealt round-robin
with --part K/4) and then merged and plotted on the laptop (--merge 4).
  split_amp_sinc_first  2026-11-06, 20:53-23:01. The shift, delay-rate and amplitude
                        terms used L = 25, the sinc term L = 10 (the process loaded before
                        the correction lags were tied to max_lag_L; sinc 10 vs 25 shows no
                        measurable difference at 0.01/0.04 Hz and 1e3/1e4 s).
  production            2026-11-09, 14:22-16:02 (--kind production).
  Per tiling: 7-51 min (split) and 7-39 min (production), growing with Delta T; most
  of it is the TD reference.

Cross-checks against figure 6
-----------------------------
A map tone at r = +-1/4 coincides with a figure 6 offset tone (+-0.25 Delta F about the
3e-4 / 3e-3 / 3e-2 Hz tones' band centres) when both land in the same band; 6 tones
coincide exactly.
  production vs the paper's fig6_bounds.npz (2026-10-28) and
  investigation_fig6_production_bounds.npz (2026-11-04):  equal to 5e-12 in log10.
  split_amp_sinc_first vs investigation_fig6_split_amp_sinc_first_bounds.npz
  (2026-11-07, all lags L = 25):  within 10% (difference from the sinc lag, L = 10 here).
Comparing each figure 6 +-0.25 tone with the map's same-offset tone in the cell that
contains it (85 pairs per model, frequencies within 0.64-1.32x): split_amp_sinc_first
agrees within a factor ~1.8; production within 2-3x, the largest gaps (up to 4.8x) at
30 mHz and Delta T = 5.6e3-1e4 s, where its error grows ~30x between the 24 and 39 mHz
cells.

What the maps show (medians)
----------------------------
  production            floor 1-4e-10 over 1e3-1e4 s (7e-11 at the lowest f); rises at high f and fine Delta T
                        (sinc at the band centre: 39 mHz 1e-8 at 1e3 s, 1e-6 at 96 s); at
                        coarse Delta T a diagonal edge with Delta T ~ 1/f (figure 5's
                        slowly-varying-delay slope): first above 1e-6 at ~1.8e5 s below
                        2 mHz, 5.6e4 s at 6 mHz, 3.2e4 s at 15 mHz; 1e-5 at 1e4 s and 2e-2
                        at 3.2e4 s for 39 mHz.
  split_amp_sinc_first  1e-12 to 1e-10 over 1e3-1e4 s; Delta T^2-like rise at coarse
                        Delta T, nearly independent of f (8e-12 at 1e4 s, 8e-11 at
                        3.2e4 s, 2e-9 at 1e5 s at low f); ~1e-9 near 0.04 Hz at all
                        Delta T. The largest-mismatch map is set by the |r| = 5/12 tones,
                        an f-independent Delta T^4 band (5e-10 at 1e4 s, 5e-8 at 3.2e4 s,
                        5e-6 at 1e5 s), so its contours run horizontally.
  Both reach ~1e-2 at Delta T = 1e6 s at low f.
